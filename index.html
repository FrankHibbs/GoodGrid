A QUICK GUIDE

How to play

Fill every square so each row, column, and outlined box contains each symbol exactly once.

Choose a square, then tap a symbol or type it on your keyboard. Turn on Notes mode to pencil in possibilities. Use Show mistakes to reveal or hide incorrect entries.

For 16×16 and 25×25 puzzles, symbols continue with letters after 9. The board scales to fit narrow screens.
